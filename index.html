A Little About Me

Memory is a story we revise every time we tell it. The words we choose decide which version sticks.

That overlap is the thing I keep working on. I went to grad school for the human side: memory, narrative, the way language reshapes thought over time. I work in AI for the model side, designing the prompts, system instructions, and evaluation frameworks behind 50+ evaluators assessing AI agent quality at Amazon, and the LLM-as-a-Judge models I've helped train to scale that work. Earlier, at MIT Solve, the same question showed up as a content system that gave one organization three distinct voices for three audiences without losing brand coherence.

AI models work the same way we do. Someone writes the words, and those words decide which version the model keeps and hands back. I'm looking for content design roles, especially in AI-driven products, where that decision is most of the work.
